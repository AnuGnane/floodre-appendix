Method in four lines

Frequency is claims notified divided by policies in force at the period midpoint, annualised. Severity is mean incurred per claim, with the median beside it. Cost per policy is incurred divided by policies in force. Loss ratio is not used: incurred is eight times ceded premium by design in a levy funded scheme, so it would say nothing about what changed.

One module, src/prep.py, holds every rule. The notebooks import it; the deck reads a JSON the code writes. The slides and the analysis cannot disagree.
