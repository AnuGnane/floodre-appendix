01 · Data quality and preparation¶

Purpose. Before trusting a single chart, find out what is wrong with the data, how big each problem is in rows and in pounds, and decide what to do about it. The brief is explicit: do not correct or exclude records silently.

Principle. Nothing is deleted. Every issue becomes a dq_* flag on the row. The analysis views (in_force, claims_valid) are explicit filters on those flags, so the effect of every exclusion is measurable and reversible. All rules live in one place: src/prep.py.

How to read this notebook. Section 1 looks at the raw file as supplied. Section 2 runs the rules and shows the full issue log. Sections 3 to 5 look at the evidence behind each issue. Section 6 reconciles raw rows to analysis rows. Section 7 states what is robust and what is not.

4. Claims file issues¶

4.1 Claims with no valid cover¶

This is the group that matters commercially. Four patterns, 42 distinct claims (a few fall under two patterns), £763k incurred, all of which are claims the scheme arguably should not be paying:

  • 12 orphans: PolicyRef does not exist in the policy file
  • 9 on policies cancelled from inception (no cover ever existed)
  • 27 notified after the policy had been cancelled
  • 1 notified after expiry

Loss date is not supplied, only notification date, so a claim notified after cancellation could be a late notification of an earlier loss. That caveat goes on the slide. It does not apply to orphans or cancelled from inception.

7. What this means for confidence¶

Robust after handling (the fix is certain and the numbers barely move): duplicates, code casing, missing risk band, bad premiums, missing dates. Portfolio trend and mix conclusions are safe.

Robust in direction, uncertain in size: claims frequency and severity. The 42 invalid cover claims and the 18 undated claims shift levels by a few percent but cannot create or remove a doubling.

Not resolvable from this file:

  • whether reserves on closed claims are real (incurred may be overstated by up to £0.6m)
  • whether Escape of water claims are in scope (£2.4m)
  • whether late notified claims relate to losses before cancellation (no loss date field)
  • why claims frequency has risen (no event, cause or weather fields)

Each of these is a recommendation, not a footnote.
